Meet the Team

Matt Smith

General Manager & Director of Golf
(555) 539-1041 matt@oconeecountryclub.com
Matt Smith is the General Manager and Director of Golf at Oconee Country Club, bringing over fifteen years of progressive leadership to the Seneca, South Carolina community. A proud graduate of Clemson University, his deep roots in the Upstate region fuel his commitment to making Oconee CC the premier club in the area. Matt earned his PGA membership in 2008 and is recognized for his balanced approach to operations, equally focused on impeccable course conditions and outstanding member services. Under his guidance, Oconee has revitalized its instructional programs, particularly its junior golf academy. Matt remains an active teaching professional, helping golfers of all skill levels refine their game, and is committed to upholding the traditions and community spirit of the club.

Jeremy Sepulveda

Greens Superintendent
jeremy@oconeecountryclub.com
Jeremy Sepulveda, CGCS, is the Director of Turfgrass Management at Oconee Country Club. A graduate of the University of Georgia's Agronomy program, he expertly manages irrigation, pest control, and sustainable practices for the South Carolina climate. Jeremy previously mastered championship-level course maintenance at highly-rated courses across the Southeast. He takes immense pride in delivering the flawless greens, tees, and fairways that members and guests enjoy daily, all while preserving the natural Oconee landscape.

George Maillot

Clubhouse Manager
george@oconeecountryclub.com
George Maillot is the Clubhouse Manager, responsible for overseeing all non-golf operations at Oconee Country Club, including dining, events, and member services. With a focus on hospitality excellence, George ensures every visit to the clubhouse is seamless and memorable. He brings a decade of high-end restaurant and private club management experience, emphasizing staff training and elevating the food and beverage program. George is dedicated to providing an exceptional atmosphere where members and guests feel welcome and valued. His leadership ensures the clubhouse remains the vibrant social heart of the club.
